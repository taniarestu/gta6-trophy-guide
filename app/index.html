    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough. There are no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals, no mission replay and no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy run. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; GTABase trophy list.

    Step 1 — Finish the story ~60–80 hours

    Play Chapters 1–5 and enjoy the game. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but any progress you make during the story already counts toward it. Do optional missions as they appear — you need any 100 of them for the checklist, and there are far more than 100.

    • Do not miss: the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude (morning after Down to Earth, Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony, choose Accept).
    • Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app (200 of 277 needed) and study animals with binoculars (100 of 255) as you go.
    • Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave, no mission replay.

    Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist ~100 hours

    After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most of it is partial counts.

    • Needed in full: all robberies (8 bank vaults, 39 gang operations), underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold medal, all drone races, Bret's 15 Target Decks.
    • Partial counts: 100 optional missions · 200/277 vehicle scans · 100/255 animals · 15/18 meditations · 70/80 challenges · 300 stunts · 100 WAiNK scans.
    • Nothing on the checklist is missable — everything stays open in free-roam.

    Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

    Stranger strands are not part of 100%, so finish the 8 qualifying strands for Strangers & Freaks (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and more being confirmed), then check the checklist for anything left. The platinum pops when the last of the 54 others does.

    Missables — only one trophy can be missed

    PowerPyx lists exactly one potentially missable trophy. Everything else can be done after the story in free-roam. The save advice below is how you protect that one trophy.

    Next up — your 3 closest trophies

      Save strategy (no mission replay)

      • There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup.
      • Make the Chapter 1 save before the morning after Down to Earth, so you can replay the Lucia balcony meditation if you walk past it.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not touch them until the platinum is yours.
      • Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent and makes police shoot on sight.
