# Leonida Platinum Guide

An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies in Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, and 46 bronze. Built for the make.fun bounty [gta6-trophy-guide-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

## What it does

- **Complete trophy list** — all 55 trophies with rarity, story chapter, and a guide written in our own words, quoting sources inline (PowerPyx, Exophase, GTABase).
- **Checklist tracker** — tick trophies off as you earn them; progress is saved locally in your browser.
- **Filter and search** — find trophies by chapter, rarity, status, or keyword; a spoiler setting hides story details until you are ready.
- **Guide pane** — every trophy card opens a step-by-step guide with source citations.
- **Roadmap** — a 3-step path to platinum in roughly 200 hours: story first, then side content and collectibles, then cleanup and the missables.
- **Missables page** — every trophy that can be missed, headed by the one missable that matters most: *Positive Mental Attitude* (the Chapter 1 meditation trophy).
- **Next-up planner** — suggests what to chase next based on your progress.
- **Export / import** — back up your checklist as a file and restore it on another device.
- **Two themes** — a warm paper theme and a dark night theme.

## How to run

No build step, no network needed — everything runs offline from this repo.

**Option 1 — open the file directly:**

Open `app/index.html` in your browser.

**Option 2 — local server:**

```sh
npx serve app
# or: python3 -m http.server --directory app
```

Then open the printed address (usually http://localhost:3000 or http://localhost:8000) in your browser.

## Sources

Guides are written in our own words, with quotations and references to the community trophy guides at PowerPyx, Exophase, and GTABase. Trophy names, rarities, and the 55-trophy set follow the official GTA VI PS5 trophy list.
